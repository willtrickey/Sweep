Two places to look

Your photo library

Where the space actually went. Every category says how it was found — exact, or a best guess.

A folder of your choosing

The big files in any folder you pick, by kind and by size — the downloads and documents a photo app never sees.

Nothing leaves your device

Sweep reads your library on device. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is shared, and nothing is deleted unless you choose it — and what you delete waits in Recently Deleted, the same as in the Photos app.

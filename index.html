What it finds

Sweep sorts your photo library into the things that actually take up room, so you can look at one kind at a time instead of scrolling through everything.

Two places to look

Photos is where most of the space goes. Files is for everything else you've pointed at it.

Your photo library

Sweep reads your library and works out where the space actually went. Every category says how it was found — some are exact, some are a best guess, and Sweep tells you which is which rather than leaving a confident number to speak for itself.

A folder of your choosing

Point Sweep at a folder and it lists the large files inside it, grouped by kind and sorted by size. Useful for the downloads and documents that never show up in a photo app.

Nothing leaves your device

Sweep reads your library on device to work out what's taking up space. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is shared, and nothing is deleted unless you choose it. Photos you delete wait in Recently Deleted, the same as deleting them from the Photos app.
